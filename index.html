ཡོན་ཏན་མཛོད · Yönten Dzö (Treasury of Precious Qualities) by Jigme Lingpa (1730–1798) is a comprehensive overview of the entire Buddhist path according to the Nyingma tradition. This explorer covers the Wisdom Chapter (ཤེས་རབ་ཀྱི་ཕ་རོལ་ཏུ་ཕྱིན་པ་བཤད་པ), the exposition of Prajñāpāramitā — the sixth and final of the Six Perfections (ཕར་ཕྱིན་དྲུག).

The chapter unfolds in three major divisions corresponding to the three types of Prajñā: hearing (ཐོས་པ), reflection (བསམ་པ), and meditation (སྒོམ་པ). The hearing section is the most extensive, covering the hermeneutical tools for interpreting scripture, the objects of knowledge, and a detailed survey of the four philosophical schools culminating in the Prāsaṅgika Madhyamaka view.

This sa bche (outline) follows the structure of the root text and its auto-commentary. Open How to use for a tour of the controls.

  • Browse the outline. Click the ▶ arrow to expand or collapse a section — the small number badge shows how many subsections it contains. Expand All / Collapse All in the toolbar open or close everything at once.
  • Mark your place. Click any row to select it. Its full path appears in the breadcrumb bar above the outline; click any step of the path to jump there. Press Esc to clear the selection.
  • Search. The search box matches Tibetan, English, and section numbers (e.g. 2.1.2). Diacritics are optional — “prajna” finds “Prajñā”. Matches are highlighted and shown together with their parent headings for context. Ctrl+F (⌘+F on Mac) jumps to the search box; Esc clears it.
  • Languages & text size. བོད་ཡིག and ENG toggle the Tibetan and English lines (at least one always stays on). − / + adjust the text size.
  • Day & night mode. The sun/moon button switches between light and dark themes. Your choice is remembered; until you pick one, the app follows your system setting.
  • Copy a path. Hover over a row and click its copy icon — or use Copy path in the breadcrumb bar — to copy the full path to the clipboard.
  • Print or save as PDF. The toolbar Print button prints the outline exactly as currently expanded. To print a single branch, hover over its row and click the print icon (or use the print button in the breadcrumb bar) — the printout is scoped to that branch, with its path shown in the page header. Choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog to export a PDF.
